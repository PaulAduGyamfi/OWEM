# Interview Script
Ask 5-10 people. Let them talk. Do not correct them. Take notes in docs/discovery/interviews/NN-firstname.md
## Questions, in this order
1. Tell me about the last time you went to dinner with a group. 2. How did the group pay?
3. Who paid initially?
4. How did you work out everyone's amount?
5. Did you split equally or by item? 6. How did you handle shared food? 7. How did you handle tax?
8. How did you handle tip?
9. How did everyone send money?
10. Did anyone forget to pay?
11. How did you remind them?
12. What was the most annoying part?
13. Have you used Splitwise / Tab / anything similar? 14. Why did you stop using it?
## NEVER ask
"Would you use an AI app that automatically splits receipts?"
This question has one socially acceptable answer, teaches you nothing, and will make you confident about a feature nobody wants. Every question above is about a past event. Past behaviour is data. Predicted behaviour is noise.
## Pay closest attention to questions 13 and 14
If most people tried Splitwise and abandoned it, THE REASON THEY ABANDONED IT IS YOUR PRODUCT SPEC.
Common answers and what they mean:
- "Typing in all the items took too long" -> an AI problem
- "I couldn't get everyone to install it" -> already avoided by
DECISION 1 (participants don't need accounts)
- "It didn't help me chase people" -> a workflow problem
## Also record, for every person
- How many people were at the dinner
- Roughly how long the splitting took
- Whether the amounts turned out wrong
- Whether they just gave up and split evenly
