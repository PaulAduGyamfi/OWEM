# Problem
When groups go to restaurants or activities, one person frequently pays the full bill and then has to manually determine what everyone owes, communicate those amounts, track reimbursements, and remind people who have not paid. This project builds an AI-assisted settlement system that digitizes receipts, helps assign expenses, calculates exact balances using deterministic logic, and coordinates reimbursement.

# Target User
The person who paid the entire bill for a group at a restaurant. Not the group. Not the person who owes. The payer. One user, one job.

# Non-goals
V1 IS NOT:
* A bank
* A Venmo/Zelle/Cash App competitor
* A social network
* Cryptocurrency payments
* A credit card product
* Full travel or corporate expense management - Autonomous money transfers
* A fleet of ten AI agents
* Multi-currency
* Recurring/subscription splitting

# Success Metrics
reference /docs/discovery/friction-analysis.md

* settlement time under 3 minutes
* zero manual calculation made by payer
* zero arithmetic errors 
* reminder follow-up requires zero payer effort

# Scope Statement
Turn a messy restaurant receipt into an accurate group settlement.
